## 📌 Project Overview

This report presents **two full digital marketing strategies** for the **World Rally Championship (WRC)**, developed using industry-standard strategic frameworks. The report addresses both a gaming audience and a streaming platform audience with distinct tactics, KPIs, and roadmaps.

> 🎯 **Goal:** Grow WRC's digital presence through structured social media and subscription strategies using data-driven analysis and strategic frameworks.

## 🎮 Part A — WRC Gaming Social Media Strategy (ENGAGE)

### E — Environment Analysis
- WRC Gaming's social media presence is significantly weaker than competitors (EA Sports, F1 Game)
- Social media drives only **5.89%** of wrc.com traffic (1.214M monthly visits)
- Instagram traffic to wrc.com is near **zero** — immediate action required
- YouTube dominates WRC social traffic at **44.67%**

### N — Needs & Audience Definition
Two target segments identified:
- **Segment 1:** Existing WRC/motorsport fans — already engaged, can be converted to gaming
- **Segment 2:** Casual gamers aged 25–45 — untapped, no prior expectations, high growth potential

### G — Goals & KPIs
- **30% follower growth** on Instagram, YouTube & TikTok within 12 months
- Monthly engagement rate tracking vs competitor benchmarks
- UGC (user-generated content) volume measurement

### A — Architecture of Content
- **Primary platform:** Instagram (visual, global reach)
- **Multi-platform:** YouTube · TikTok · Twitch
- Content mix: Gameplay trailers · Cinematic reels · Behind-the-scenes · Driver stories · UGC reposts
- Monthly content calendar with weekly themed weeks

### G — Governance & Community
- Community managers for moderation and engagement
- Gamified challenges, leaderboards & weekly skill contests
- Influencer ambassador programme
- Clear community guidelines from day one

### E — Evaluation & Optimisation
- Social listening: Hootsuite & Sprout Social
- Monthly KPI reviews: reach, engagement rate, follower count, CTR
- A/B testing for content types and posting times
- Quarterly community surveys for qualitative feedback

## 📺 Part B — Rally.tv Digital Marketing Strategy (SOSTAC)

### Situation Analysis
| Metric | Data (Feb 2026) |
|--------|----------------|
| Monthly Visits | 363,774 |
| Global Rank | #123,484 |
| Motorsport Industry Rank | #121 |
| Bounce Rate | 37.53% |
| Mobile Traffic | 52.88% |
| Social Media Share | 9.39% |
| YouTube dominance of social | 88.27% |

### Objectives (SMART — 2026–2031)
- **40% year-over-year** subscription growth by 2028
- Improve conversion rate through UX optimisation
- Reduce churn through personalised content
- Reach **top 50** motorsport industry ranking by 2031

### Strategy
- Dual focus: **Acquisition + Retention** equally weighted
- Value proposition: *"The world's only dedicated WRC streaming platform — live action, documentaries and behind-the-scenes exclusively"*
- Three target audiences: WRC fans · General motorsport fans · WRC Gaming crossover audience

### Tactics
- **Acquisition:** CTV ads on Smart TVs · Paid social on Instagram, YouTube, TikTok · Influencer partnerships · Free trials · Bundle with gaming subscriptions
- **Conversion:** Optimised landing pages · Strong CTAs · Limited-time promotions
- **Retention:** AI-driven content recommendations · Behavioural segmentation · Exclusive content drops · Regular platform feature updates

### Action Plan (Phased)
- **Phase 1:** Reduce churn via UX improvements and early AI integration
- **Phase 2:** Rich content strategy + revenue diversification (sponsorships, bundles, merchandise)
- **Phase 3:** Global expansion and market capture — position Rally.tv as the #1 dedicated rally platform

### Control
- Monthly KPI reviews: subscription growth, churn, conversion, engagement
- Quarterly deep-dive performance reviews
- Social listening tools for brand monitoring
- Net Promoter Score (NPS) surveys
- Regular stakeholder alignment sessions

## 📊 SWOT Summary — Rally.tv

| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|-----------|
| Only platform for exclusive WRC content | Low brand awareness globally |
| Direct-to-consumer streaming model | Only 363K monthly visits |
| Loyal niche fanbase | Instagram traffic near zero |

| Opportunities | Threats |
|--------------|---------|
| Growing demand for niche sports streaming | YouTube dominates with 27B visits |
| Untapped Instagram & TikTok audiences | DAZN & Amazon Prime competition |
